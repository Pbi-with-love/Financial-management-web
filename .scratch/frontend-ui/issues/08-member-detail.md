# 08: Member detail page

**What to build:** Member detail page at `/members/:id`. Header shows member name and computed balance (red if negative). Two sections (tabs or stacked): Deposits and Item Shares. Deposits section lists that member's deposits with amount, date, note, voided badge. Item shares section lists items the member was charged for, showing item description, parent expense title, share amount, and date. Loading/error states.
