# 07: History page

**What to build:** History page at `/history`. A unified chronological list of expenses and deposits, sorted by date descending. Expense rows show title, formatted total, date, and item count. Deposit rows show member name, formatted amount, and date. Voided records display with a "Voided" badge and strikethrough styling. Tapping an expense row expands it inline to show its items with descriptions, amounts, and per-member shares. Loading/error states.

**Blocked by:** 01-scaffolding, 02-api-client
